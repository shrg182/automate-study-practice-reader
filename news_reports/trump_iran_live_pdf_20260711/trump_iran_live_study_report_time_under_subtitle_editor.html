English Learning Vocabulary rolling coverage: Continuous news reporting that is repeatedly updated as events develop. live stream: Audio and video transmitted over the internet while an event or broadcast is taking place. editorial headline: A headline whose wording reflects the publisher's framing or interpretation of a story. direct quotation: The exact words spoken or written by a named source. attribution: Information identifying who made a statement or supplied a claim. ceasefire: An agreement or order to stop fighting, either temporarily or permanently. retaliatory strike: A military attack presented as a response to an earlier attack. escalation: An increase in the seriousness, intensity, or scale of a conflict. sanctions designation: An official action placing a person or entity under specified economic restrictions. nuclear negotiations: Diplomatic talks concerning nuclear activities, limits, inspections, or related sanctions. freedom of navigation: The principle that ships may travel through international waters in accordance with international law. Strait of Hormuz: A narrow waterway connecting the Persian Gulf with the Gulf of Oman and an important route for global energy shipments. auto-generated captions: Speech-to-text subtitles created automatically by software rather than prepared and checked by a human editor. timestamp: A time marker showing where a statement or segment begins in a recording. metadata snapshot: A saved record of a video's title, publisher, status, and other details at a particular time.
