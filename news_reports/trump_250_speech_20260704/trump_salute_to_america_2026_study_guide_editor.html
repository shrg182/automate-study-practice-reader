Overall Structure and Tone The speech combined three main registers:  Ceremonial: honoring the 250th anniversary, veterans, historic flags, and the founding generation.  Campaign-style: attacking ideological opponents and promoting voting-law changes.  Spectacular: using military flyovers, fireworks, historic objects, and staging to intensify the message. For reading practice, notice where the speech moves from ceremony to politics and then back to patriotic spectacle. This change of register is one of the most important features of the event. 5.
