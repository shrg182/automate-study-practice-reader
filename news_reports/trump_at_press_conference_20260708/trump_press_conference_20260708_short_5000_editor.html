Very Short Summary The speaker said NATO countries should spend more on defense and buy more American-made equipment. He said the United States was respected again. Reporters asked about Iran, communism, oil, European allies, Türkiye, and the F-35 program. A useful study point is the difference between policy words, such as defense and alliance, and emotional words, such as great, terrible, dangerous, and crazy. Study Goal Do not try to learn every word. First, understand the event, the repeated topics, and the speaker's main claims. Then use the transcript to practice listening for dates, numbers, countries, and verbs like will, might, want, need, and think.

Key Listening Excerpts Excerpt A: NATO and defense spending [03:39] "We achieved an unprecedented agreement to increase the annual defense spending ... from 2% to 5% of GDP." Plain English: He says NATO members agreed to spend more money on defense. Listen for numbers: two percent, five percent, GDP. Excerpt B: American-made equipment [07:14] "They all want American-made equipment ... the Patriots and the Tomahawks ... are considered to be the best." Plain English: He says other countries want weapons made in the United States. Useful phrase: American-made means made in America. Excerpt C: Communism question [25:21] A reporter asks about "the dangers of communism" and people who may not understand "how close communism has come to home." Plain English: The reporter asks for a message to people living under communist or socialist systems, and also to people in democracies. [26:04] "Communism is a disaster ... it has never worked and it is not going to work." Plain English: The answer uses very strong judgment words. Notice the repeated pattern: it has never worked; it is not going to work. Excerpt D: Türkiye and the F-35 [38:28] "They are talking about the F-35 airplane ... I have not totally made up my mind." Plain English: He says the F-35 issue is still not finally decided. This is a good example of cautious language: not totally made up my mind.

Vocabulary for 5000-Word Learners Word Simple meaning Example summit a meeting of leaders The NATO summit was in Ankara. alliance countries working together NATO is a military alliance. defense protection from attack They talked about defense spending. target a goal or number to reach The spending target was five percent. equipment tools or machines for a job The speaker praised U.S. military equipment. factory / plant a place that makes products He said more plants were being built. tariff a tax on imported goods Tariffs were linked to jobs and factories. communism a political system opposed in the answer The reporter asked about communism. socialism a political idea discussed with communism He warned about socialism. relationship how people or countries are connected He described ties with Turkey. decision a choice after thinking He said no final F-35 decision was made. Useful Sentence Patterns • We need to + verb: We need to produce it faster. • I think + sentence: I think they are making progress. • It is going to + verb: It is going to happen very fast. • not sure / not totally: I am not sure I want to make a deal. • because of + noun: That is because of the tariffs.
