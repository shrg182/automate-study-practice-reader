# Source acquisition and import protocol

## Target edition

The source is the later 91-volume horizontal simplified edition of 《毛泽东批注二十四史》. A complete set is reported at Yale University Library. The catalog identifier, holding location, use conditions, and reproduction policy still need confirmation.

## Minimum request for the 《史记》 pilot

Request the following before requesting complete-volume photography:

1. Title and copyright pages for the set.
2. The complete table of contents for the three 《史记》 volumes.
3. Ten representative pages containing printed annotation images or editorial annotation blocks.
4. One continuous chapter of approximately 20–40 pages.
5. Permission and technical conditions for research reproduction.

These materials are sufficient to confirm pagination, image quality, annotation placement, and the relationship between the three physical volumes and the 130 traditional juan.

## Import requirements

Each acquired file receives:

- source institution and catalog identifier;
- physical history and source-volume number;
- original filename, byte size, MIME type, and SHA-256 checksum;
- reproduction date and rights/use note;
- printed page label and image sequence number;
- OCR status and confidence;
- annotation anchors and transcription-review status.

The open Chinese Text Project Wuyingdian scan is a comparison source only. It must never be displayed or described as a scan of Mao's annotated edition.

## Offline packaging

Catalog metadata and research citations are installed by default. Page images and OCR are packaged independently by history or source volume. Private notes and reading state remain when an image package is removed.
