# Anti-Dühring Reader

English-first close-reading edition of Frederick Engels’ *Anti-Dühring*, with section-aligned Chinese reference support.

The initial five Readers cover the prefaces, both introduction chapters, and the first two chapters of Part I. The selection page links to the complete English and Chinese tables of contents and PDFs.

Rebuild downloaded sources with `python3 build_book.py --refresh-clean`; rebuild from saved text with `python3 build_book.py --local`.
