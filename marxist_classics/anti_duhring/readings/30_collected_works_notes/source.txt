Marxists Internet Archive: MECW File No Longer Available Marx-Engels Archive “File No Longer Available!” The file you have tried to access was transcribed from the Marx Engels Collected Works. Lawrence & Wishart , who hold the copyright for the Marx Engels Collected Works, have directed Marxists Internet Archive to delete all texts originating from MECW. Accordingly, from 30th April 2014, no material from MECW is available from marxists.org. English translations of Marx and Engels from other sources will continue to be available. However, files which existed on marxists.org prior to April 2014 are still available on MIA mirrors which are not controlled by MIA.
