# Marxist Classics Reading Plan

## Purpose

After the *Shiji* reading stage, this project will study nine Marxist classics while a separate project continues the close reading of *Capital*. The two projects should inform one another, but they should not be merged into a single reading sequence.

*Capital* should remain the slower and more technical political-economy track. The works in this project form a faster companion track concerned with historical orientation, philosophical method, political programs, imperialism, the state, revolution, and political strategy. A working allocation is approximately 70 percent of close-reading time for *Capital* and 30 percent for this series.

## Recommended sequence

### Phase 1: Orientation and foundations

1. 《共产党宣言》
2. 《费尔巴哈与德国古典哲学的终结》
3. 《哥达纲领批判》

《共产党宣言》 supplies the broad historical and political map. 《费尔巴哈与德国古典哲学的终结》 then introduces the philosophical background and Engels's retrospective account of the development of the materialist conception of history. 《哥达纲领批判》 applies this framework to a concrete political program and provides an important discussion of distribution, transition, and different phases of communist society.

Primary-text references:

- [Engels, *Ludwig Feuerbach and the End of Classical German Philosophy*](https://www.marxists.org/chinese/marx-engels/21/030.htm)
- [Marx, *Critique of the Gotha Programme*](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/download/Marx_Critque_of_the_Gotha_Programme.pdf)

### Phase 2: System and political economy

4. 《反杜林论》
5. 《帝国主义是资本主义的最高阶段》

《反杜林论》 ranges across philosophy, political economy, and socialism. On the first pass, read the introductions and the political-economy and socialism sections closely; review the detailed natural-philosophy controversies more selectively. A complete second reading can follow later. 《社会主义从空想到科学的发展》, which Engels developed from chapters of 《反杜林论》, may be used as a short preview rather than as a substitute for the full work.

《帝国主义是资本主义的最高阶段》 should be coordinated with the accumulation and concentration portions of *Capital*. Its progression from monopoly and banking to finance capital, capital export, division of the world, and imperialism's historical position makes it the strongest bridge between the two projects.

Primary-text references:

- [Engels, *Anti-Dühring*](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/download/pdf/anti_duhring.pdf)
- [Lenin, *Imperialism, the Highest Stage of Capitalism*](https://www.marxists.org/archive/lenin/works/1916/imp-hsc/index.htm)

### Phase 3: State, revolution, and strategy

6. 《国家与革命》
7. 《无产阶级革命和叛徒考茨基》
8. 《共产主义运动中的“左派”幼稚病》

These three works should be read as a connected sequence. 《国家与革命》 reconstructs Lenin's interpretation of Marx and Engels on the state. 《无产阶级革命和叛徒考茨基》 applies that position in a polemic against reformism and competing interpretations of revolution. 《“左派”幼稚病》 then considers the opposite strategic danger: treating refusal of compromise, parliamentary activity, established organizations, or work among broader masses as a universal principle.

Primary-text references:

- [Lenin, *The State and Revolution*](https://www.marxists.org/ebooks/lenin/state-and-revolution.pdf)
- [列宁：《无产阶级革命和叛徒考茨基》](https://www.marxists.org/chinese/lenin/marxist.org-chinese-lenin-1918-10.htm)
- [列宁：《共产主义运动中的“左派”幼稚病》](https://www.marxists.org/chinese/lenin/192004/index.htm)

### Phase 4: Consolidation

9. 《论马克思、恩格斯及马克思主义》

Use this work as a capstone and reference collection. Because publications under this title may contain different selections, inspect the table of contents of the chosen edition before dividing it into reading units.

## Coordination with *Capital*

| Topic in *Capital* | Companion work |
| --- | --- |
| Commodity, value, money, and the historical role of capitalism | 《共产党宣言》 |
| Labor, exploitation, surplus value, and distribution | 《哥达纲领批判》 |
| Machinery, industrial production, and systematic exposition | Political-economy sections of 《反杜林论》 |
| Accumulation, concentration, monopoly, and finance | 《帝国主义是资本主义的最高阶段》 |
| State power and political transition | 《国家与革命》 |
| Organization, reformism, revolutionary tactics, and mass politics | 《无产阶级革命和叛徒考茨基》 and 《“左派”幼稚病》 |

The table is a conceptual crosswalk, not a requirement for exact chapter-by-chapter synchronization. The works were written at different levels of abstraction and in response to different historical controversies.

The two reading projects should share the project dictionary, a reference library, a people-and-organizations index, a historical timeline, and a concept crosswalk. Each individual book should retain its own text corrections, annotations, footnotes, pending questions, and reading log. This preserves connections without treating historically distinct arguments as one undifferentiated doctrine.

## Review principles

- Separate an author's argument from the historical evidence offered for it.
- Record the immediate controversy and intended audience of each work.
- Distinguish terms that retain the same meaning across works from terms whose usage changes.
- Compare primary texts before relying on later summaries.
- Mark disagreements and changes among Marx, Engels, Lenin, Kautsky, and later interpreters rather than harmonizing them automatically.
- Revisit this plan after completing the first three works and after reaching the accumulation sections of *Capital*.
