髳，中国古代少数民族名。曾参与牧野之战。

The wording “鹿类动物；古书猎获清单中的兽名” was my concise annotation, not a verbatim dictionary definition.

It was based on:

- Dictionaries define **麈**, pronounced **zhǔ**, broadly as a large deer-like animal: [教育部《国语小字典》](https://dict.mini.moe.edu.tw/SearchIndex/word_detail?breadcrumbs=Zhuyin_%E3%84%93_%E3%84%93%E3%84%A8%CB%87&otherList=D0003831%2CD0001830%2CD0002342%2CD0003741%2CD0004305%2CD0002268%2CD0002959%2CD0004360&wordID=D0004360) and [漢典](https://zdic.net/hant/%E9%BA%88).
- “古书猎获清单中的兽名” comes from its Chapter 25 context in 《逸周书·世俘解》: **“貉十有八，麈十有六，麝五十……”**

Because historical dictionaries differ on its exact modern zoological identity, “鹿类动物” was intentionally broad. A more transparent annotation would be:

> 古书所载的一种鹿类大兽；本章猎获清单中作“麈十有六”。
